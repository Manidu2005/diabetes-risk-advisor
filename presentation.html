🎯 Problem Statement

The Challenge

  • ● Diabetes affects 537 million adults globally (IDF, 2021)
  • ● Nearly 50% of cases remain undiagnosed
  • ● Early detection can prevent or delay complications
  • ● Lifestyle changes can reduce risk by up to 58%

Our Solution

A hybrid AI system that combines three AI techniques to:

  • ✅ Predict diabetes risk from health metrics
  • ✅ Categorize risk with expert-informed fuzzy logic
  • ✅ Recommend personalized lifestyle changes
  • ✅ Provide an interactive web interface for easy access

📊 Dataset — Pima Indians Diabetes

Source & Details

  • 📁 Source: NIDDK / UCI ML Repository
  • 📝 Records: 768 patients
  • 📐 Features: 8 health metrics + 1 outcome
  • 👥 Population: Pima Native American women, age ≥ 21

Data Preprocessing

  • 🔄 Replace biologically impossible zeros with NaN
  • 📊 Impute missing values with column medians
  • ⚖️ StandardScaler normalization
  • ✂️ 80/20 stratified train-test split

Features

Pregnancies — Number of pregnancies
Glucose — Plasma glucose concentration (mg/dL)
BloodPressure — Diastolic blood pressure (mm Hg)
SkinThickness — Triceps skin fold (mm)
Insulin — 2-Hour serum insulin (μU/mL)

🌫️ Stage 2 — Fuzzy Logic Engine

Why Fuzzy Logic?

Neural networks output a number — but health risk isn't black and white. Fuzzy logic adds nuance by modeling "degrees of truth".

Example: A BMI of 29.5 is partially "overweight" and partially "obese" — fuzzy logic handles this naturally.

Input Variables (Antecedents)

Risk Probability (0–1.0) → low / medium / high
BMI (0–60) → underweight / normal / overweight / obese
Activity Level (0–10) → sedentary / moderate / active

Output Variable (Consequent)

Risk Category (0–100) → Low (≤25) / Moderate (≤50) / High (≤75) / Severe (>75)

36 Fuzzy Rules

Full combinatorial coverage: 3 × 4 × 3 = 36 rules

IF risk=low AND BMI=normal AND activity=active THEN category=Low
IF risk=medium AND BMI=overweight AND activity=moderate THEN category=Moderate
IF risk=high AND BMI=obese AND activity=sedentary THEN category=Severe
⋮ (33 more rules)

Membership Functions

  • 📐 Trapezoidal (trapmf) — for boundary categories
  • 📐 Triangular (trimf) — for middle categories
  • 🔄 Overlapping boundaries for smooth transitions
  • ⚙️ Centroid defuzzification method
  • 🛡️ Input clamping to avoid edge-case crashes
  • 🔙 Fallback if no rules fire: risk_prob × 100

📋 Stage 3 — Rule-Based Engine

How It Works

Translates the fuzzy logic output into actionable, personalized recommendations using deterministic if/elif logic.

Low Risk — 4 maintenance recommendations
Moderate Risk — 4 proactive recommendations
High Risk — 5 urgent recommendations
Severe Risk — 5 critical recommendations

Always Includes

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer — Always the first recommendation, emphasizing this is a screening tool, not a diagnosis.

6 Metric-Specific Modifiers

Additional recommendations are added based on individual health metrics:

🩸 Glucose — Extra advice if > 140 or > 180 mg/dL
⚖️ BMI — Guidance for underweight (<18.5), obese (>30), severely obese (>35)
💓 Blood Pressure — Alerts if > 90 or > 100 mm Hg
💉 Insulin — Special advice if > 200 μU/mL
🎂 Age — Additional screening for > 45 or > 60
👨‍👩‍👦 Family History — Heightened alerts if score > 0.5 or > 0.8

~25–30

distinct recommendations contextually selected per patient

⚠️ Limitations & Ethical Considerations

Limitations

📊 Dataset Bias

Trained exclusively on Pima Native American women from a 1960s study — may not generalize to men, other ethnicities, or younger populations.

📏 Limited Features

Only 8 health metrics — real clinical assessment uses many more factors (HbA1c, fasting glucose, lifestyle, etc.)

📉 Small Dataset

Only 768 records — modern clinical AI uses thousands or millions of records.

Ethical Safeguards

🏥 Not a Diagnosis

Framed explicitly as an educational screening tool — disclaimer is always the first recommendation.

🔒 Privacy

No real patient data stored. All inputs are processed locally and not persisted.

⚖️ Error Awareness

Acknowledges potential for false positives/negatives — always recommends professional consultation.
